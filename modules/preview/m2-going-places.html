The hook
"One verb — IR — lets you talk about the present AND the future from day one."
Core concepts
IR conjugation (voy / vas / va / vamos / van)
IR + a + infinitive = near future (voy a comer)
a + el = al · question: ¿A dónde vas?
Key vocabulary
voy a + infinitivo vas a + infinitivo al restaurante ¿A dónde vas? ¿Qué vas a hacer?
Voy a ir a la biblioteca mañana.
I am going to go to the library tomorrow.
Practice drill
Translate: I am going to eat. / Are you going to go to the restaurant?
Culture note — IR is wildly irregular — but also the most-used verb. Master it first and half your conversations are covered.
